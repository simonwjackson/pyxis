Pyxis design reference

Working pages, not mockups. Real library data — 370 albums, 3,865 tracks and every cover — so long titles, dark artwork and 244-album density behave the way they will in the product. Listening history, placements and offline availability are synthetic.

Surfaces

StacksHome The collection as a wall of covers. Shelves for what is in rotation, untouched and never played, then search, sort, filters and the full grid. DiscoveryInbox One album at a time. Keep, later or no. The captured track appears only as the reason the album is here. SearchAcquire Reaches installed sources. Anything added lands in Discovery. The one surface that cannot work offline. HistoryJournal Every play, grouped by day, with the room it happened in. One line of insight, no charts. RoomsSheet Where the sound is and how to move it. Opens from the room control in the now-playing bar, not from navigation. SourcesSetup Where music comes from. Connecting, signing back in, installing. Reached from the account control, the only chrome that is about you rather than music. DevicesSetup Everywhere sound can come out, this one included, and the code that adds another. SystemTokens Colour, type, and the components the surfaces are built from, in both themes.

States

Themes

Light Dark

Decisions these pages encode. The album is the subject everywhere and tracks are demoted or hidden. The now-playing bar is permanent and expands into the player, so playback is a layer rather than a destination. Rooms is a sheet because it is consulted rarely. Offline availability is set by placement with a per-album override, and having an album is shown separately from wanting it.

Not answered here. Nothing is styled for a brand: no logo, no illustration, no colour beyond the covers. There is no artist page and no playlist, because the album is the unit, and no queue editor.
