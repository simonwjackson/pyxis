System

One typeface, hairlines instead of cards, and covers as the only source of colour. Heat is reserved for one meaning: something is playing, or something is waiting on you.

Colour

--groundPage. Warm near-black in dark, near-white in light.
--raisedInputs and the now-playing bar.
--inkText, and the fill for primary actions.
--softSecondary text. Never for anything interactive alone.
--ruleHairlines. Used instead of cards and shadows.
--heatPlaying, or awaiting your decision. Nothing else.

Dark is not an inversion. It shifts warm rather than blue, text is off-white to avoid glare, and --edge adds a hairline inside every cover — without it, black album art has no boundary against the page.
