Prototypes

Three answers to “what is the home screen.” One visual system. The album is the subject everywhere; tracks are demoted or hidden.

  1. A

    Discovery

    One album at a time. Keep, later, or no.
  2. B

    Stacks

    A wall of covers. In rotation, untouched, never played.
  3. C

    Rooms

    Every room, what it is playing, and how to move it.

Real: 370 albums, 3,865 tracks, and all 370 covers, pulled from the live library and the public source RPC.
Invented: placements, play counts and dates. The real library is 370/370 in Discovery with no listening history.
