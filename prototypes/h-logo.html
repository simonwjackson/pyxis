Four candidates, drawn in the product's own vocabulary: a square is a cover, the heat dot means sound. Shown at the sizes a launcher actually uses, on the grounds we do not control, and inside the circle Android crops to.

The first draft used the interface's own hairlines, which is wrong: a 16px stroke at 512 is under two pixels on a home screen and gone in a favicon. An icon is not a small drawing, it is a shape that has to survive being one. These are weighted for 48px and judged there.
